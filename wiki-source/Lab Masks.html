Lab Mask is a gear item in Abiotic Factor.

The lab mask has nine color variants, all changing the color of the visor. These colors are clear, red, pink, yellow, orange, green, blue, purple, and black.

Effects

Lab Mask allows the player to negate the harmful gas/spore effects. It also reduces absorbed radiation by 15%.

Sources

Lab Mask can only be obtained through finding it in a few locations.

Locations

Cascade Laboratories

  • At level 2, close to the Holding Cell, in a room upstairs that's close to some sinks.
  • At level 2, in a room upstairs from the lobby, close to the toilets.
  • At level 2, in the Wildlife Pens.
  • At level 3, in Adjustment.
  • In the Control Center, close to where the Leyak Essences need to be placed.
  • In the Control Center, close to where the Leyak Essences need to be placed, upstairs from the kitchen.
  • In the Control Center, close to a Barrel and some Cement and some pipes.

Rise

  • In the first level.

Hydroplant

  • In the Waterfall Apartments, in the room where the Voidpack recipe is found.
  • Outside of the Hydroplant Building, in a small room with a level 5 keypad and a ladder leading down.

Voussoir

  • Five can be found throughout the first building.

Reactors

  • Multiple Lab Masks can be found throughout the Dusk, Gale and Cloud Reactors areas.
  • A unique green Lab Mask can be found within the Dusk Reactor area, inside the Medical Clinic, next to a Neophyte.
  • A unique pink Lab Mask can be found within the office room of the Dusk reactor, on a desk beside a Photocopier, with a Volatile Exor patrolling the cubicles.
  • A unique yellow Lab Mask can be found within the bathroom of the Dusk Reactor, on the woman's side in the same offices as above.
  • A unique black Lab Mask can be found behind two chairs in the corner of the side room in the Dusk Reactor office area, the off shoot past the kitchen area with another Volatile Exor patrolling the halls.
