Collectibles are deployable objects that can be found in various locations. They are often small statues, bobbleheads, figurines & toys that depict a certain group of NPCs, enemies or vehicles. Some of them have a small interaction when placed down, otherwise their only function is decoration.

Most collectibles are directly related to the area they are found in, such as a specific portal world or sector.
