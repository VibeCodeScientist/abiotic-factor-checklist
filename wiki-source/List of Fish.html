Not to be confused with the Fishing Skill.
Players can fish in almost any body of water in Abiotic Factor.

Fishing is a gameplay mechanic and minigame added in the "Crush Depth" Update in Abiotic Factor. It allows the player to catch various fish in most locations where water is present.
