Armor Sets

From Abiotic Factor Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

There are several Armor Sets in Abiotic Factor. Most armor sets have a set effect that activates when two, three, or all parts of the set are worn. Some armor sets may provide basic protection at first, but can be upgraded using an Enhancement Bench to improve their armor or add additional bonuses.
