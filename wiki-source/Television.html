A Television or TV is a deployable furniture item that can be found throughout several locations. These televisions play certain videos and audio, some for informing the player on what to do, others are just color bars. Most televisions in the facility currently are unique and plays a particular video only.

There are a total of 11 different TVs.

Locations

TV: Power Your Science

Can be found in the Office Sector, in the small resting area close to the cafeteria kitchen.

TV: Resting is Essential

Can be found in the Office Sector level 2, just the next to the Security Office and near to the Maintenance Lift to Level 3.

TV: Portal Worlds

Can be found in the Office Sector level 3, in the Silo 2 Lounge.

TV: Forklift Certification

Can be found in Manufacturing West, in the kitchen next to the Supply Room.

TV: Trams, Trams, Trams!

Can be found in Manufacturing West in the Lot Oversight's small office, near to the Foundry where The Blacksmith is.

TV: Standby

Can be found in Manufacturing West in the Alloy Handling building directly up the stairs. Just near to the large gate.

Another one can be found in Cascade Laboratories in the Containment Block Baird, where you'll first encounter Dr. Cahn. The cell will unlock further into the story after the security system is reset.

One can also be found in Reactors, in the "This is a black clearance sector" area, in the "Waygate Control" room, accessible after disabling the electricity.

TV: Immurement and You

Can be found in Cascade Laboratories. It is located in the lobby at level 2.

TV: Channel 5

It is found in the Furniture Store.

TV: Gatekeepers: Allies in Science

Located in a locked office in the Dusk Reactor, accessible through an air vent hidden behind two crates, then heading through a fan that turns off at night.

TV: Static

It is located in Shadowgate within IS-0117s containment area, tucked away in a corner near a Military Cot by the entrance to the garage area.

TV: ???

It is located in Residence Sector within the barracks.

While this TV usually shows a static picture of an eye, occasionally it displays a mouth which opens and then returns to the eye.
