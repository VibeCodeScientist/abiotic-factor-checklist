Photo Frames

From Abiotic Factor Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Photo Frames are deployable objects that can be found in various locations. Photo Frames depict various cats and dogs for the most part, but also GATE personnel and other portraits.

All photo frames have the same "scientist looking up" inventory icon, but different names, descriptions and pictures when deployed.
