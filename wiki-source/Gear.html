Survival in Abiotic Factor hinges on your ability to confront paranormal and humanoid threats head-on, and your crafted arsenal is your best line of defense. As elite researchers, players have the unique opportunity to use their scientific knowledge to fashion an array of weapons and ammunition. From improvised tools to advanced weaponry, mastering the art of crafting is crucial for your team's survival.
