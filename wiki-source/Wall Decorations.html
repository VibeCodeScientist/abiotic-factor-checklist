Wall Decorations

From Abiotic Factor Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Wall Decorations are deployable paintings, posters, signs and other decorations that can be found in various locations. Some wall decorations are unique and can only be collected from a single location but most others are found at multiple instances or even in Portal Worlds where they can repeatedly be picked up.

A paintings inventory icon does not always match the deployed painting and the icon is often reused.
