Lab Mask locations for the checklist, written in our own words based on a Reddit community guide. Format: a short area line, then one line per mask: "Colour- where to find it". Masks spawn by chance; if one is missing, wait for the world and the portal world to reset.

Hydroplant Snow Area / Voussoir

Orange- Ride the elevator up and fight past the guards to reach the top area. Go through the door into a dark room; the orange mask is in there. You can leave through the door with the green sign above it.

Clear- From where the orange mask was, turn left and walk straight on to the counters; the clear mask lies on a counter. Clear masks also turn up all over the facility.

Blue- From the clear mask's counter, take the door on the right, turn left and keep going to a large counter with the blue mask. The door with the green sign above it also leads there.

Red- Further into Voussoir, take the stairs up to the highest part of the building. In the room where a neutrino mapper normally lies during the story, the red mask can appear in its place. Reportedly the hardest one to get.

Purple- From the red mask's room, cross to the opposite side of the building. A small room in the middle of that side holds the purple mask.

Reactors / Dusk Reactor

Green- In the Dusk Reactor's main area (where a flying gatekeeper usually is), face right and walk to the big green medical sign. Continue straight to a door, go in and turn right; the mask lies on a table next to an enemy.

Pink- In the Dusk Reactor's main area, face left and go up into the area with the bookshelves. Go through the door there and on to the cubicle area. Kill the Volatile Exor first - its explosion can destroy or bug the mask. The pink mask is on a table on the far side of the cubicles that face left.

Black- From the pink mask, climb onto the cables along the ceiling and head left into a tiny room with a steel cable, then drop through the floor (watch out for another patrolling Volatile Exor). In the room on the left with two couches, a lamp, a rug and a Pest on the table, the black mask sits behind the chair.

Yellow- Back in the cubicle area, look for the bathrooms; the yellow mask is in the women's bathroom.
